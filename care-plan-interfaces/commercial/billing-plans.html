Plan Lengths

Define the commercial commitment durations — 3, 6, or 12-month subscriptions — with payment frequency and recurring cycles. Plan lengths are independent of clinical protocols and consumption rates.

ⓘ
Delinked from Clinical Plans: Billing plans define how the patient pays (duration, frequency). They do NOT dictate what medication is sent or when — that's governed by the Clinical Treatment Plan and Consumption Rate. A patient on a "6M Monthly" billing plan may receive medication every 4 or 6 weeks based on their clinical consumption rate.

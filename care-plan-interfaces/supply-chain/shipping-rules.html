Shipping Rules

The Shipping Service orchestrates the delivery schedule — it knows when a shipment is due (based on clinical consumption rate) and what to pack. These rules define the logistics layer.

⚠
Consumption-Driven Shipping: The "Next Ship Date" is calculated from the Clinical Portal's Consumption Rate, NOT the billing cycle. A patient billed monthly may receive shipments every 4 or 6 weeks. The Shipping Service simply responds to: "When does this patient's current supply run out?"

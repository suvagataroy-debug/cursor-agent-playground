Logistics Rules Engine

Create automated inclusion rules that determine what gets packed with each shipment. E.g., "If Medication = Injectable, then append SKU: Free Sharps Bin" to the manifest. Rules are evaluated at fulfilment time and automatically build the shipment manifest.
